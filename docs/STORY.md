# INTERSTATE '51 — Story Bible

> *The atomic age has a speed limit. Nobody told them.*

## The world

**Nevada, spring 1951 (alternate history).** The Atomic Energy Commission has
just started setting off bombs on the flats of the new Nevada Proving Grounds.
Down in Las Vegas the casinos throw "dawn bomb parties" so guests can watch the
flashes from the rooftop pools.

Between the test site and the Strip lies Nye County: a basin of dry lakes and
Joshua trees ringed by bare, red mountains. Two-lane highways run through it
with nobody watching them. Men who came home from the war with a mechanic's
skills and no job have bolted surplus machine guns onto their hot rods. Some
ride shotgun for the truckers, and folks call them *outriders*. The rest ride
for money. The biggest outfit in the county is the **Chrome Legion**, and it
belongs to one man.

## Characters

| Character | Who they are | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Wade "Deacon" Calloway** | Our driver. Drove for the Red Ball Express across France in '44 and came home to a mechanic's job in Bakersfield. Quiet and dry-humored. He is very dangerous behind the wheel of **Black Sally**, a chopped '49 Mercury. | Narrates the briefings and journal |
| **Hollis "Holly" Calloway** | Wade's little brother, a long-haul trucker. He took night runs for Vale Freight, saw what was really in the crates, and disappeared. | Radio |
| **Rosa Delgado** | Hollis's girl. She was a WASP ferry pilot during the war and now runs the **Boneyard**, a salvage yard and garage on the dry lake. She is Deacon's mechanic, quartermaster and voice on the CB. | Radio |
| **Elijah "Preacher" Tate** | An old bootlegger-preacher out of the Gold Creek ghost town. He runs gas, guns and gospel across three counties in a hearse called the **Glory Wagon**. | Radio |
| **Dr. Miriam Holt** | A British physicist on loan to the Proving Grounds. She found that the plutonium cores don't add up. | Radio |
| **Augustus Vale** | Casino owner (the Starlight Crown), freight baron and master of the Chrome Legion. Smooth and patient, and he never raises his voice. Drives the **Silver Duchess**. | Radio |
| **Sheriff Boyd Harlan** | Nye County's law, on Vale's payroll. His cruiser is called **The Judge**. | Radio |
| **Colonel Rusk** | Head of security at the Proving Grounds. He signs whatever Vale puts in front of him. | Intercepted radio |
| **Hal** | Runs Hal's Eats at Tonopah Junction, a truck stop with the best pie on 95. | Radio |

## The plot

Vale has been stealing plutonium cores from the Proving Grounds. Colonel Rusk
falsifies the manifests, and Vale Freight trucks carry the cores to the
abandoned **Silver Queen** mine. Hollis drove those trucks, figured it out and
was locked up in the mine. Vale's last move is to hijack the core meant for
tomorrow's dawn test, **Shot Able**. The Army will set off a dummy, and Vale
will fly the real bomb out of Indian Springs while every eye in Nevada is on
the mushroom cloud.

## Campaign

| # | Mission | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Boneyard** | Afternoon | Deacon arrives at Rosa's yard. Rosa puts him through his paces: run the flags on the lakebed, then shoot up some junkers. Three Legion scouts arrive and he wrecks them. Rosa tells him Hollis was last seen at Tonopah Junction. |
| 2 | **Tonopah Junction** | Noon | The Legion is burning Hal's truck stop. Deacon drives them off. Hal points him to Hollis's burned rig, and the logbook inside shows night runs from Mercury to the Silver Queen, with AEC crates and Vale's signature. Reinforcements come down 95. |
| 3 | **Sheriff's Welcome** | Dusk | Sheriff Harlan's roadblock waits in the Gold Creek canyon. There is a stand-off, then a firefight, then deputies close in from behind. Before he dies, Harlan gives up a name: *the Colonel*. |
| 4 | **Glory Wagon** | Golden hour | Deacon meets Preacher at Gold Creek and escorts his convoy through the canyon to the Boneyard, fighting off three Legion waves. Rosa picks up a mayday on the military band. |
| 5 | **Chain Reaction** | Dawn | Dr. Miriam Holt is running south on 95 with the Legion on her bumper. Deacon saves her and brings her in through an ambush. Her story: three plutonium cores went out and only two came back. |
| 6 | **Lights Out at Mercury** | Night | A raid on Vale's freight depot. Deacon takes down the radio mast before the alarm goes out, blows the fuel farm and steals the dispatch records. The records say Hollis is alive at the Silver Queen. Deacon overhears Vale and Rusk on the radio. |
| 7 | **The Silver Queen** | Morning | Deacon and Preacher storm the mine: the guard towers, then the gate. Hollis breaks out in his own rig, "Bessie", and Deacon escorts him to safety. Hollis reveals the plan: the real core rides a convoy tonight. |
| 8 | **Broken Arrow** | Dusk | Deacon runs down the armored Juggernaut before it reaches Indian Springs. It turns out to be a decoy full of sandbags. "The house never shows its cards." |
| 9 | **Atomic Dawn** | Dawn | Vale's Silver Duchess makes a run for his plane with the real core, the whole Legion around it and everyone on the radio. H-minus three minutes. The bomb goes off on the horizon in the middle of the chase. Deacon ends it. |

**Epilogue:** The Army called it a training exercise. Rusk was reassigned to a
weather station in Alaska. Miriam went home with enough paper to bury half the
Pentagon. Hollis and Rosa got married in the Gold Creek church, and Preacher
did the honors. Deacon is still out there, riding shotgun.

## Tone references

Interstate '76's funk-noir road movie, moved back to the atomic 50s:
spaghetti-western twang, jump-blues and rockabilly, the Civil Defense
posters, the neon of early Vegas, and the chrome of Detroit iron.
